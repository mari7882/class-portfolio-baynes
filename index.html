Amaria is a senior at Michigan State University majoring in Information Science within the college's department of Communication Art & Sciences.

About

I am a graphic designer where I have experience of creating logos, social media content, and customized clothing and branding for companies and start-ups. I am also a student in MI 349 at Michigan State University where I am learning hands-on skills such as creating a website and getting more experience with website design.

I currently have a graphic design business, GBM Design (aka Graphics By Mari), where I make customized clothing, accessories, marketing, website design, YouTube Editing, and social media branding for entrepreneurs, content creators and brick-and-mortar businesses.

Projects

Website Design & Branding

With GBM Design, I create graphics for businesses. For websites, I create designs for people or businesses to use on platforms and website domains such as Shopify, GoDaddy, and Squarespace. These design packages often include brand logos, flyers, holiday promotional marketing, and photo/video editing. When designing the website, I update any products or articles needed and design it using custom color schemes. I utilize the branding I've created to design the website. I also have experience with Adobe, which helps with the design process.

Custom Apparel & Accessories

I am very experienced in creating custom clothing such as t-shirts, hoodies, hats, and shoes. I create designs and clothing for entrepreneurs looking to expand their brands as well as many campus organizations at Michigan State University.

Youtube & Editing

In addition to creating website designs, I also edit videos and create motion graphics for YouTubers. I can create YouTube Introductions and outros, thumbnails, and banners.

Current Projects

Currently, I am learning more about website design and how to create websites in MI 349. I am also learning how to use 3D printers and how to bring ideas to life using different tools and prototyping methods. I've most recently created a prototype for a wind-up toy.

Career Path

Since attending Michigan State University, Media and Information has always interested me, and I've learned more about careers in UI/UX design. I've always been interested in how things work and always looked for ways to improve things. Since I've been designing graphics, I have developed an interest in learning how websites and apps are created behind the scenes and how designers know what tools to use for users. The more I strengthen my technical skills, I believe UI/UX design would be a great career path, and I aspire to work with companies like Instagram (Meta) and Google.
